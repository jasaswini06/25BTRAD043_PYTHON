Campus Verification & Claim Protocol

01. Physical Check

Every logged item is deposited at the Central Security Desk within 2 hours of report. No unverified third-party handovers allowed.

02. Identity Match

For ID cards, laptops, and wallets: Claimant must present active university ERP credentials or original student RFID ID card.

03. Custody Log

Security registers the claimant's Roll Number and logs a signed custody transfer form before changing item status to "Claimed".
